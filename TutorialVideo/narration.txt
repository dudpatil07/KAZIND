Welcome to the Drone Threat Simulator. Start by choosing a mission. Civilian Tracking has three targets. Mixed Airspace adds a military aircraft. Full Threat Response includes all six drones.

In the field, move with W, A, S, and D, and aim with your mouse. Center the crosshair on a drone and press F, or click Lock Aim. Keep it centered for ten seconds. If your aim slips, the lock resets.

The drone will be brought down. You then have ten seconds to identify it. Check the airframe and signature, then choose Civilian or Military. You can also press J for Civilian or K for Military.

Correct calls earn one hundred points. Wrong calls cost fifty. If time runs out, the drone escapes. Identify every target to complete the mission. Good luck.
